Clinical Services & Specialties

Evidence-Based Psychiatric & Therapeutic Care

We provide comprehensive outpatient psychiatry, conservative medication management, and specialized trauma-informed psychotherapy for adults, adolescents, and families across Indiana.

Specialized Outpatient Programs

Every patient pathway is led by board-certified physicians and licensed clinical therapists dedicated to conservative dosing and deep emotional healing.

Diagnostic Assessment

Comprehensive Psychiatric Evaluation

In-depth biological, psychological, and lifestyle assessments to build a diagnostic foundation without rush. We examine family history, previous therapies, and laboratory biomarkers to uncover underlying roots.

Format: 60-Minute Intake · In-Person or Telehealth
Clinician: Board-Certified Psychiatrist (Dr. Elena Vance, MD)
Schedule Evaluation
Pharmacotherapy

Conservative Medication Management

Evidence-based pharmacotherapy tailored to your unique biology, prioritizing the lowest effective dosage and continuous monitoring. We de-prescribe unnecessary compounds and optimize safety.

Format: 30-Minute Monitoring Sessions · Monthly / Bi-monthly
Focus: Depressive Disorders, Anxiety, ADHD, Bipolar Spectrum
Explore Medication Care
Psychotherapy

Integrative Psychotherapy & EMDR

Individualized CBT, ACT, and psychodynamic counseling addressing trauma, anxiety, mood disorders, and life transitions. Specialized somatic and EMDR modalities for nervous system integration.

Format: 50-Minute Weekly or Bi-weekly Sessions
Clinician: Marcus Reed, LCSW (EMDR Certified)
Request Therapy Session
Youth & Families

Adolescent & Family Support

Specialized psychiatric guidance for young adults navigating anxiety, ADHD, and emotional regulation. We coordinate directly with families and academic support systems to nurture sustainable resilience.

Ages: Adolescents (14–17) and Young Adults (18–24)
Modality: Family Systems & Cognitive Development
