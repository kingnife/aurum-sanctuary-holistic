Common Questions

Clarifications on Care & Intake

Common questions regarding our New Albany clinic, appointment protocols, and telehealth consultations.

What should I expect during my initial psychiatric evaluation?
Your initial evaluation is an unhurried 60-minute consultation. Your physician will review your developmental background, psychological symptoms, previous medical treatments, and current lifestyle factors. Together, we establish an accurate diagnostic foundation.
How does conservative medication management differ from standard prescribing?
Conservative medication management prioritizes the lowest effective dose of scientifically validated compounds, avoiding polypharmacy and excessive prescription reliance.
Where is your physical clinic located in New Albany?
We are located in the historic medical district at 120 E. Market Street, Suite 300, New Albany, IN 47150, convenient to both Floyd County and downtown Louisville.

Crisis Safety Protocol

If you or a loved one are experiencing an acute mental health crisis, please dial or text 988 to connect immediately with the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 for an emergency.

Care Center is an outpatient facility; this service is not for emergencies.
