Financial Transparency

Insurance Coverage & Clear Fees

We prioritize absolute clarity regarding medical billing. Whether utilizing in-network insurance or out-of-network reimbursement, you receive transparent financial terms before your first visit.

Direct In-Network Partnerships

Care Center is contracted directly with major commercial healthcare plans and Medicare. Our billing team files claims directly on your behalf.

Anthem Blue Cross / Blue Shield

In-network for commercial employer plans, individual marketplace exchange policies, and Medicare Advantage across Indiana.

Aetna Behavioral Health

Full coverage for outpatient psychiatric evaluations, conservative medication management, and psychotherapy.

Cigna Healthcare & Evernorth

Direct participating provider status for outpatient behavioral health services in-person and via telehealth.

Humana Commercial & Medicare

Covered for specialized physician consultations and licensed psychotherapy with transparent co-pay verification.

Traditional Medicare Part B

Direct assignment accepted for eligible beneficiaries seeking outpatient psychiatric diagnosis and pharmacotherapy.

UnitedHealthcare & Optum

Comprehensive behavioral health network participation across Floyd County and statewide telehealth.

Out-of-Network Superbill Concierge

If your plan is with a PPO insurer outside our network, our billing concierge automatically generates itemized, clinical CMS-1500 compliant receipts (Superbills) with required diagnostic CPT codes.

Most PPO plans reimburse 60% to 80% of eligible out-of-network outpatient psychiatric and therapy fees.

Crisis Safety Protocol

If you or a loved one are experiencing an acute mental health crisis, please dial or text 988 to connect immediately with the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 for an emergency.

Care Center is an outpatient facility; this service is not for emergencies.
