HIPAA Compliance & Patient Privacy

Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

1. Our Clinical Commitment to Privacy

Care Center LLC ("Care Center", "we", "us", "our"), located at 120 E. Market Street, Suite 300, New Albany, IN 47150, is dedicated to maintaining the privacy and security of your Protected Health Information (PHI). Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and applicable Indiana state law, we are required to furnish you with this formal Notice explaining our legal duties, privacy practices, and your individual rights concerning your medical and mental health records.

2. Permissible Uses and Disclosures

We may utilize and disclose your PHI without prior written authorization in the following clinical circumstances:

  • Treatment: Providing, coordinating, or managing your psychiatric evaluation, medication management, and psychotherapy sessions, including consultation between Dr. Elena Vance, MD, and Marcus Reed, LCSW, or coordinated care with your designated primary care physician.
  • Payment: Generating claims, verifying in-network coverage (e.g., Anthem Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, Humana, Medicare Part B, UnitedHealthcare), or preparing automated itemized superbills for reimbursement from your insurer.
  • Healthcare Operations: Quality assessment, auditing clinical outcomes, accreditation, and managing business operations necessary to maintain clinical excellence.
  • As Required by Law: Complying with federal, state, or judicial mandates, such as judicial subpoenas or court orders.
  • Imminent Threat to Health or Safety: In urgent situations where disclosure is necessary to prevent a serious, imminent threat to your safety or the safety of another individual, in accordance with Indiana professional licensing regulations.

3. Psychotherapy Notes Protection

Notes recorded by our therapists documenting the contents of a private counseling session or a group, joint, or family counseling session that are kept separate from the rest of your medical record receive heightened protection under HIPAA. They are not released to insurance companies or third parties without your explicit, separate written authorization, except where mandated by strict legal exceptions (such as defense in legal proceedings or mandatory reporting of child/elder abuse under Indiana law).

4. Your Individual Rights Under HIPAA

As a client of Care Center, you maintain the following legally enforceable rights:

  • Right to Inspect and Copy: You have the right to inspect and obtain an electronic or paper copy of your mental health record and billing file within 30 calendar days of written request.
  • Right to Amend: If you believe medical information in your record is inaccurate or incomplete, you may submit a formal request for amendment.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You may request a list of certain non-routine disclosures of your PHI made by our practice.
  • Right to Request Confidential Communications: You have the right to specify how and where our clinic contacts you (e.g., cell phone only, discrete voicemail, or alternate mailing address).
  • Right to Restrict Disclosures: If you pay for an outpatient service out-of-pocket in full, you may instruct us not to disclose that encounter to your health plan.
  • Right to a Paper Copy: You may request a physical copy of this notice at any time during your clinic visit.

5. Security and Electronic Transmission

All electronic communications, including our telehealth platform, intake portal, and electronic health record (EHR) databases, utilize 256-bit TLS encryption, dedicated access controls, audit logs, and SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud hosting. Routine unencrypted email is discouraged for clinical exchanges; clients are invited to utilize our encrypted patient portal.

6. Privacy Officer & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this notice or wish to file a privacy grievance, please contact our designated Privacy Officer:
