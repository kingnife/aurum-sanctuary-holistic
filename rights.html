Patient-Centered Charter

Patient Rights & Responsibilities

At Care Center, healthcare is an active, equal partnership. We are committed to fostering a therapeutic environment grounded in mutual respect, autonomy, and transparency.

1. Your Rights as a Patient

Every client receiving care through Care Center has the right to:

  • Dignity & Respect: Receive compassionate, respectful, and culturally affirming care free from discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
  • Informed Consent: Receive comprehensive, clear explanations regarding your psychiatric diagnosis, proposed medication protocols, therapeutic modalities, potential risks, side effects, and viable alternatives prior to initiating treatment.
  • Active Participation: Actively collaborate with your physician (Dr. Marcus Vance, MD) and psychotherapist (Sarah Lin-Holloway, LCSW) in developing, reviewing, and modifying your individualized care plan.
  • Confidentiality: Expect strict privacy regarding your medical discussions and protected health records, adhering to HIPAA and Indiana state mental health confidentiality laws.
  • Right to Refuse Treatment: Decline or discontinue any specific intervention, medication, or therapeutic modality, and be fully informed of the clinical implications of such a decision.
  • Financial Transparency: Receive clear, upfront fee explanations prior to appointments in accordance with the No Surprises Act, including Good Faith Estimates for uninsured or self-pay services.

2. Your Responsibilities as a Patient

To ensure the highest quality of therapeutic care, clients are asked to:

  • Provide Accurate Information: Share complete and honest information regarding your psychiatric and medical history, current prescriptions, over-the-counter supplements, and substance use.
  • Engage in the Treatment Plan: Inform your clinician promptly if you experience unexpected side effects, wish to adjust a dosage, or feel a therapeutic modality is not serving your goals. Never abruptly stop psychiatric medications without medical consultation.
  • Respect Appointment Times: Arrive promptly for in-person appointments and log in to the virtual waiting room 5 minutes prior to scheduled telehealth sessions. Provide at least 48 business hours' notice if you need to reschedule.
  • Maintain Telehealth Privacy: Connect to virtual visits from a private, secure, and quiet location where you feel comfortable speaking freely and without driving or engaging in unsafe activities.
  • Fulfill Financial Obligations: Settle copayments, coinsurance, or self-pay fees promptly as agreed upon during intake.

3. Resolving Concerns & Grievances

If at any point you feel your rights have not been honored, or if you have concerns regarding your care, we encourage you to discuss them directly with your clinician or submit a formal concern to our Practice Administrator. We take all feedback with utmost gravity and will investigate and respond promptly.
