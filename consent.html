Virtual Care Clinical Standards

Informed Consent to Telehealth Services

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. This document outlines the clinical protocols, security measures, and regulatory considerations governing video consultations with Care Center clinicians across Indiana.

1. Nature of Telehealth Consultations

Telehealth involves the delivery of healthcare services using interactive, encrypted audio and video communications. At Care Center, telehealth enables you to receive comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, conservative medication management, and evidence-based psychotherapy from Dr. Elena Vance, MD, or Marcus Reed, LCSW, without traveling to our New Albany clinic.

2. Indiana Licensure & Geographic Requirement

Under Indiana Code IC 25-1-9.5 and licensing regulations of the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (PLA), healthcare providers must be actively licensed in the state where the patient is physically located at the time care is delivered. Therefore, you must be physically present within the State of Indiana during every virtual session. At the start of each consultation, your clinician will verify your exact physical address.

3. Emergency Protocols & Local Contact Designation

Because your clinician is not physically present in your room, telehealth requires established emergency safety protocols:

  • You agree to provide the clinic with a local emergency contact person and your exact physical location at the beginning of each session.
  • In the event of an acute medical or psychiatric emergency during a session, your clinician has the professional duty and authority to contact local emergency dispatch (911) or your designated emergency contact.
  • Care Center telehealth appointments are strictly outpatient; this service is not for emergencies. For life-threatening emergencies or acute crises between sessions, dial 988 or 911 immediately.

4. Technological Security & Client Environment

Our virtual waiting room and video sessions operate over high-definition WebRTC channels secured with end-to-end 256-bit encryption compliant with HIPAA and HITECH standards. No video or audio of sessions is ever recorded or archived by Care Center. To protect your own confidentiality, you are required to participate from a private, enclosed space free from interruptions or unauthorized third parties. Driving or operating machinery during a virtual session is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination of the visit.

5. Technical Interruptions & Reconnection Protocol

If a video connection is disrupted due to internet instability or equipment failure, your clinician will attempt to reconnect via the virtual waiting room for 2 minutes. If reconnection fails, your clinician will call you immediately at your primary telephone number on file to either complete the visit by phone or reschedule without penalty.

6. Clinical Discretion & In-Person Transition

While telehealth is clinically effective for most outpatient needs, certain conditions may warrant physical in-person examination or hands-on clinical assessment. If your treating physician or therapist determines that virtual care is no longer clinically appropriate or safe for your specific condition, we will coordinate transitioning your care to our downtown New Albany clinic or refer you to an appropriate local facility.
